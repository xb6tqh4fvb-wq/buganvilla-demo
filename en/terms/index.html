Terms

Free preview: we build a preview of your new website at no cost and with no obligation. It stays online for one week.

Prices are in euros and exclude VAT. The build is a one-time fee. A yearly plan (Hosting & maintenance, or Updates) is required and billed per year. Marketing packages are optional and billed per year. We never charge monthly.

A change is one message with an adjustment to text, a photo, a price or opening hours on an existing page. A new page, a new language or a new feature is quoted separately. Changes included in a yearly plan expire at the end of the plan year; a bundle of 10 changes is valid for 12 months.

If you end your yearly plan, we hand over the files of your website on request.

If you approve the preview within one week, we send an invoice and put the site live. Without approval, the preview is taken offline and you owe nothing.

You keep all rights to your own texts, images and logo. Rights to the finished design pass to you once the build invoice is paid.

Our liability is limited to the amount you paid us in the past 12 months. Dutch law applies.
