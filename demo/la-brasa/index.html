Our story

A chef, a fire and a corner of Russafa.

Chef Marta Soler grew up in Alzira, between orange groves that her grandfather pruned every winter. The cuttings went straight into the family grill. When she opened La Brasa, she brought that habit with her: the grill uses Valencian orange wood and holm oak, which burns hot and leaves a soft, sweet smoke.

The menu is short on purpose. Marta buys at the Mercat de Russafa three mornings a week, two streets away, and the chalkboard changes with what she finds. Fish comes from the Lonja of Dénia, vegetables from the huerta north of the city, wine from small cellars in Utiel-Requena and the Marina Alta.

“Fire is honest. You can't hide anything from it, so you start with the best ingredient and then you mostly leave it alone.”

— Marta Soler, chef & owner
