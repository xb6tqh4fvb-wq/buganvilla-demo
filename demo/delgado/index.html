What we build

Four trades, one team, no subcontractor roulette.

Our own crew of builders, plumbers, electricians and tilers works on every job. That is how we keep quality steady and timings honest.

Kitchens

Layout redesign, cabinetry, stone and quartz worktops, plumbing, electrics and lighting. From the first sketch to the last silicone joint.

Typically 3–5 weeks on site

Bathrooms

Walk-in showers, underfloor heating, microcement or porcelain, new drainage and ventilation. Accessible bathrooms on request.

Typically 2–3 weeks on site

Full home renovations

Apartments and villas stripped back and rebuilt: new distribution, electrics to current REBT standard, plumbing, floors, carpentry and paint.

Typically 8–14 weeks on site

Terraces & pools

Waterproofing that actually lasts, porcelain decking, pergolas, outdoor kitchens and pool renovation or new-build plunge pools.

Typically 3–8 weeks on site

How we work

Four clear steps. You always know what happens next.

  1. 01

    Visit & measure

    We come to the property, listen, measure and take photos. No cost, no obligation. If you live abroad, we can do the first visit with you on a video call.

    Within 7 days of your request

  2. 02

    Fixed written quote

    An itemised quote in English or Spanish: materials, labour, timings and payment stages. The price you sign is the price you pay, unless you change the brief.

    5 working days

  3. 03

    Permits & planning

    We prepare and submit the licence for the works with the ayuntamiento, order long-lead items and agree a start date that suits you.

    2–4 weeks, depending on the town hall

  4. 04

    Build & handover

    One site manager runs your job from day one. Weekly photo updates, a clean and tidy site, and a final walk-through with a written snag list.

    Agreed in the quote, week by week
