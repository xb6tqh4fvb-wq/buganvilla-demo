Why Litoral

A local office, not a call centre.

We are a small team on the Avinguda del Rei Jaume I. You'll deal with the same person from the first call to the keys.

  • Sales and rentals under one roof

    Buying a second home and renting it out when you're away? We handle the purchase, and afterwards the bookings, tourist licence, cleaning and check-ins.

  • Four languages at the table

    English, Spanish, Dutch and German are spoken in our office, so nothing gets lost between you, the seller and the notary.

  • Every step to the notary

    NIE number, Spanish bank account, an independent lawyer of your choice, the deposit contract and the deed — we keep the whole checklist moving.

  • We know the streets

    We live here. We'll tell you which street gets the afternoon sun, where parking is impossible in August, and which views are protected.

Areas we cover

From Albir to Calp, and the villages behind.

  • Altea

    Whitewashed old town, the blue-domed church and a pebble seafront lined with restaurants.

  • Altea Hills

    Gated hillside with contemporary villas and wide views over the bay.

  • Albir

    Sandy-coloured promenade, the Serra Gelada natural park and an easy, international feel.

  • Calp

    The Peñón d’Ifac, long beaches, a working fishing port and modern apartments.

  • Polop & La Nucía

    Inland villages ten minutes from the coast — more land and quiet for your money.
