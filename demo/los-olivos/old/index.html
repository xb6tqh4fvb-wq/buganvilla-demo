Bienvenidos - Welcome - Willkommen

Welcome to the web of Finca Los Olivos!! Our rural house is situated in the beautiful Serrania de Ronda, only 4 km from the town of Ronda, between olive trees. Enjoy the tranquility of the nature, the swimming pool and our typical andalusian breakfast. We have 8 rooms all with bathroom.
