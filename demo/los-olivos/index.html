Experiences

Andalusian cooking class

Salmorejo, Ronda-style oxtail and almond cake with Carmen in our farmhouse kitchen. Lunch is what you cook.

Thu & Sat · 3 hours · €65 p.p.

Olive oil tasting

Taste our own early-harvest oil next to two neighbours’ and learn why the green, peppery one is the prize.

Daily at 18:00 · 1 hour · €20 p.p.

Hiking the Serranía

Walking routes from the gate: the old mule track to Arriate, or the longer loop past the cork oaks. Maps and picnic on request.

5 to 18 km · free route cards

Ronda & the gorge

Ten minutes by car to the Puente Nuevo and the 100-metre Tajo gorge. Go early, before the day-trippers, and walk down to the old mills.

10 min · we lend you a parking card

Breakfast & the long table

Toast, tomato, our own oil.

Breakfast is the Andalusian way, and then some: pan de pueblo from the bakery in Arriate, grated tomato, our early-harvest oil, local goat's cheese and jamón, fruit from the trees by the pool, eggs any way you like and proper coffee.

Three evenings a week Carmen cooks a set dinner at the long table under the vine: four courses, seasonal and simple, with wines from the Serranía de Ronda.

Getting here

Closer than it looks on the map.

Fly into Málaga, rent a car at the airport and take the A-357 and A-367 inland. The last stretch climbs through the Serranía: drive it in daylight the first time, it is half the holiday.

Good to know

Questions guests often ask.

Anything else? Write to Carmen and Javier at stay@fincalosolivos.example. We answer within the day.

What time are check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 15:00 to 21:00, check-out until 11:00. Arriving later? Let us know and we leave a key box code and a cold plate in your room.

Is breakfast included?

Yes, breakfast is included in every room rate and served from 8:30 to 11:00 on the terrace, or by the fireplace in winter.

Do I need a car?

We recommend one. The finca is 4 km outside Ronda on a good gravel track. We can arrange a taxi from Ronda station, around €15.

Are children welcome?

Children from 8 years are welcome in all rooms. Younger children stay with us in Casa del Pastor, which has its own fenced garden.

When is the pool open?

The saltwater pool is open from 1 May to 15 October, 9:00 to 20:00. Towels and loungers are by the pool, quiet hours after 14:00.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 14 days before arrival. Within 14 days we charge the first night, unless we can re-let the room.
