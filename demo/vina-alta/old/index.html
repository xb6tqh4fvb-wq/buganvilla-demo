Bodega Viña Alta is a family winery situated in Haro, in the heart of the Rioja Alta. Since 1921 our family elaborates wines of great quality with the grapes of our own vineyards. We have wines Blanco, Rosado, Joven, Crianza, Reserva and Gran Reserva.

For visits to the bodega please contact by telephone or fax with minimum 1 week of anticipation. Groups only. Visits in spanish.
