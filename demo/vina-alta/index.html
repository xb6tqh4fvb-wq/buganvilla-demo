Our story

A small cellar, a long memory.

In 1921 Tomás Sáenz planted three hectares of Garnacha on the stony terraces above the Ebro, on land nobody else wanted because it was too high and too windy. That wind is exactly what gives our grapes their freshness.

Today his great-granddaughter Lucía makes the wine and her brother Iñaki looks after the vines. We still harvest every bunch by hand into small crates, still age our Reserva in the stone cellar the family dug beneath the house, and still believe a wine should taste of the place it comes from.

“We only make what the vineyard gives us. Some years that means less wine. It never means lesser wine.”Lucía Sáenz, winemaker
  1. 1921Tomás plants the first Garnacha terraces above the Ebro.
  2. 1958The family digs the stone cellar beneath the house by hand.
  3. 1994First wine bottled under the Viña Alta name.
  4. 2019All vineyards converted to organic farming.
