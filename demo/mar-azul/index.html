Your language, at every step

From the phone call to the treatment chair to the invoice: you are helped in English, Spanish, Dutch, German or French. Treatment plans and aftercare instructions are written down in your language too, so you can read them again at home.

Insurance accepted

We work with most private Spanish health insurers and many international expat policies. Not sure about yours? Send us your policy name and we check your cover before your visit. Paying yourself? You receive an itemised invoice you can claim back.

Your first visit

Four calm steps. No surprises.

Nervous about the dentist? Many of our patients were. Tell us when you book and we plan extra time, explain every step before we do it and agree a stop signal.

Plan my first visit
  1. 1

    Book online or by phone

    Choose a treatment and a preferred moment. We confirm within one working day, in your language.

  2. 2

    A short welcome form

    We send a simple health questionnaire beforehand, so your visit starts with a conversation, not paperwork.

  3. 3

    Examination & explanation

    We look, scan and show you what we see on screen. You ask everything you want to know.

  4. 4

    Your written plan

    You leave with a plan and a fixed quote. Take it home, think it over, decide in your own time.
