The house

Thirty years of paella in the old town.

Casa Eulogio has been on Calle Manyans, in the heart of Valencia’s old town, for over 30 years. A classic casa de comidas, where lunch or dinner is always a pleasure.

Every day we make fresh paella valenciana and seafood paella, for lunch and for dinner, along with a wide choice of tapas, combination plates and desserts.
