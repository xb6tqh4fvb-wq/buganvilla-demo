The restaurant

Valencian cooking, at the pace of the Albufera.

Arrocería Maribel sits in the heart of the Albufera Natural Park, in El Palmar, 18 km from Valencia. Chef María José Estevens starts from the traditional recipe and refines it with technique, always with rice as the star.

Most of our suppliers of fruit, vegetables, meat, fish and rice are from El Palmar, the Albufera and the surrounding villages. Everything is made fresh every day in our own kitchen.
